ARCHITECTURE DECISIONS

Decisions, not buzzwords.

The design goal is a low-maintenance site that stays accurate and useful to both casual visitors and professional recruiters.

01 / SOURCE OF TRUTH

A structured master CV

Professional roles, achievements, dates and credentials should be stored as verifiable records, not regenerated from a PDF every time.

02 / AI WITH SOURCES

Evidence before fluency

Answers and role-specific CVs should use reviewed source material, clearly distinguishing documented facts from generated wording.

03 / RIGHT-SIZED CLOUD

Serverless by default

Optimise for low operational burden and appropriate security rather than deploying heavyweight services for a modest personal site.
